How the templates work

  1. Open a template to see its themes and examples.
  2. Choose Use this template to open the preview in Slateory.
  3. Name the new project and confirm. Your current projects stay unchanged.
  4. Edit the suggested ideas, add your own and choose one to work on.

Free planning without an account

You do not need a YouTube connection to use a template. New projects have no connected channel or analytics. Your content stays in this browser; export a backup when you need a copy. There is no automatic synchronization between devices.

Prefer your own plan? Open the board, create your themes and add ideas with their demonstrations and viewer value.
