FREE CONTENT PLANNING TEMPLATE

Building in public planning board.

Explain the real decisions behind a product, with evidence and limitations.

This free starter contains three themes and four editable ideas. The examples below are exercises, not claims about your work, published videos or results. Replace them with material you can demonstrate from your own experience.

Use this template →

Problem & scope

Show who the work is for and what it solves.

One user, one frustrating moment

What to show: Recreate a specific workflow using fictional data. Show the awkward step and sketch one smaller alternative.

Value for viewers: Viewers see how to scope a product around a concrete problem.

Production note: Distinguish your assumption from observed user evidence. Do not invent interviews.

Build decisions

Make a trade-off concrete with a working example.

The feature I deliberately left out

What to show: Compare a small working flow with a sketch containing one extra feature. Explain its cost and what you would need to learn first.

Value for viewers: Viewers learn how to make a practical scope decision.

Production note: Use a decision you actually made; otherwise label this as a hypothetical exercise.

Two versions of the same interaction

What to show: Walk through two prototypes of one action, such as adding an item. Show the steps and explain which constraint each version serves.

Value for viewers: Viewers can evaluate an interface using a concrete task.

Production note: Do not imply a usability win unless you tested it. Show the trade-off.

Tests & lessons

Share what happened, including what did not work.

The edge case that changed the design

What to show: Use a reproducible case such as an empty list, a long name or a failed request. Show the failure and the revised behavior.

Value for viewers: Viewers learn to test beyond the happy path.

Production note: Use synthetic data. Include one limitation that remains after the fix.
