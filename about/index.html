ABOUT SLATEORY

A content planner for YouTube and Instagram.

Slateory helps individual creators turn a collection of video ideas into a plan. Group YouTube videos, Shorts and Instagram Reels by theme, see what is in progress, and keep completed work below a shared divider.

Plan around your themes

Create your own columns and add an idea’s demonstration, viewer value, notes and format. Search the board when you are ready to choose your next video.

Keep progress visible

Mark a video as in progress or complete. A moving border highlights work in progress; finished cards stay available below the board.

Add YouTube context

Optionally connect your channel to show public posting dates and video counts. Owner sign-in adds scheduled uploads and dated, read-only analytics.

Your workspace stays in your browser

The planner is free to use and needs no Slateory account. Your ideas are saved in this browser’s IndexedDB, rather than uploaded to a Slateory server. Export a JSON backup to move your board to another device or keep a copy. Clearing browser data can remove your work.

Does Slateory publish or schedule videos?

No. It is a planning workspace. You publish on YouTube or Instagram yourself. The optional YouTube calendar displays dates fetched from your channel; it does not create scheduled uploads. There is no Instagram API connection.

Do I need a YouTube API key?

Only for the optional public YouTube connection. Planning works without a key. Private channel reports and scheduled uploads require your own Google OAuth client and read-only sign-in. Google API usage is subject to Google’s quotas and terms.
