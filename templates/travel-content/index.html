FREE CONTENT PLANNING TEMPLATE

Travel content planning board.

Plan useful travel stories around real footage and clearly dated information.

This free starter contains three themes and four editable ideas. The examples below are exercises, not claims about your work, published videos or results. Replace them with material you can demonstrate from your own experience.

Use this template →

Plan the day

Help viewers make one practical travel decision.

One neighborhood, a realistic half-day route

What to show: Map a short route using places you visited. Pair each stop with your own footage and separate walking time from time spent there.

Value for viewers: Viewers can judge whether the route fits their pace and interests.

Production note: Name the location and visit date. Verify current access and opening hours before publishing.

On the ground

Use real footage to show what a visit feels like.

What the scenic clip does not show

What to show: Pair a scenic shot with the approach, steps, crowd or waiting area from the same visit.

Value for viewers: Viewers can assess the effort and conditions behind the view.

Production note: Describe your actual visit. Conditions vary by season and time; avoid universal claims.

The same place at two times of day

What to show: Compare footage from two actual visits with visible timestamps or a clearly labeled visit note.

Value for viewers: Viewers can compare atmosphere and plan a time that suits them.

Production note: Only use this idea if you have both recordings. Do not present a single visit as a recurring pattern.

Useful details

Answer the questions a pretty montage leaves open.

What this outing actually cost

What to show: Build an itemized breakdown from your own receipts or notes. Separate optional spending and costs shared with others.

Value for viewers: Viewers get a transparent example they can adapt to their own budget.

Production note: State date, currency and party size. Hide personal payment details and verify any current prices you quote.

Make this board your own

The button opens a preview in Slateory. Name and confirm the new project before anything is saved. All four ideas start as Suggested; edit them, choose a format and mark work In progress when you start production. Completed cards move below the shared divider.

Your existing projects stay unchanged. No YouTube connection is copied into this project, and planning works without an API key. Content is stored in this browser, with JSON export and restore for backups. It does not automatically sync to your phone or another computer.
